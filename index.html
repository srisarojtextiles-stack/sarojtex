Our story · Since 1992

A Legacy of Trust, Tradition & Timeless Fashion

Established in 1992 · Bargur, Krishnagiri, Tamil Nadu

Mr. Ramesh Duraisamy
Founder · 1992

The story of Sri Saroj Textiles began in 1992, when our founder, Mr. Ramesh Duraisamy, established the business in Bargur, Tamil Nadu, with a simple yet meaningful vision: to make quality clothing and beautiful traditional textiles accessible to every family at affordable prices.

What started as a humble business venture gradually grew into a trusted name among textile shoppers in Bargur and surrounding regions. Built through years of dedication, personal relationships, consistent quality and a deep understanding of customers' needs, Sri Saroj Textiles has proudly carried forward its commitment to serving generations of families.

Today, under the proprietorship and leadership of Mrs. Sumathi Ramesh, Sri Saroj Textiles continues this proud legacy, combining traditional values with evolving fashion trends and modern customer expectations.

Our Heritage, Your Trust

Located in Bargur – one of the well-known textile and clothing market destinations in Krishnagiri district, Tamil Nadu – Sri Saroj Textiles brings together an extensive and carefully selected collection of ethnic wear, sarees and fashionable clothing.

We believe that looking beautiful and dressing elegantly should never come at an unreasonable price. That is why we focus on offering:

  • Beautiful Saree Collections: A vibrant range of traditional, contemporary and fashionable sarees for every occasion.
  • Quality You Can Trust: Carefully selected textiles that balance comfort, durability and style.
  • Affordable Pricing: Attractive prices that make quality fashion accessible to every customer.
  • Festive & Special Occasion Wear: Elegant collections for weddings, festivals, family functions and celebrations.
  • Customer-First Service: Friendly assistance and a shopping experience built around individual needs and satisfaction.

More Than a Textile Store – A Family Legacy

For us, Sri Saroj Textiles is not just a showroom; it is a legacy built on relationships, trust and commitment.

From our humble beginnings in 1992 to the growing collections we offer today, our journey has always been guided by one promise: every customer deserves quality, style and value for their money.

As fashion evolves and new trends emerge, we continue to embrace change while preserving the traditional values on which our business was founded.

Three decades of trust. Generations of customers. One promise – Quality, Affordability and Satisfaction.
